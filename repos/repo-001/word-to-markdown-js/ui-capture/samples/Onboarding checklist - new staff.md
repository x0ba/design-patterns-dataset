# New Staff Onboarding Checklist

## Week 1

- Laptop and accounts (Google Workspace, Slack, dispatch dashboard)
- Read the Driver Handbook, even if you won't drive
- Ride along for one full shift in each zone
- Lunch with your onboarding buddy

## Week 2

- Shadow dispatch for a morning peak (6–9 a.m.)
- Review the last two quarterly board reports
- Set up 1:1s with Marcus and Priya

## By day 30

- Complete CPR/first aid certification (we pay)
- Present one improvement idea at the ops sync
