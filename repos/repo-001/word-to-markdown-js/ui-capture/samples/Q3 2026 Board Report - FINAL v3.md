# Q3 2026 Board Report

_Prepared by Priya Raman for the October 21 board meeting. Figures are unaudited._

## Summary

Ridership on the three pilot microtransit zones grew **18.4%** quarter over quarter, driven mostly by the Eastside zone after the September schedule change. We closed the quarter \$41,200 under budget, largely because the second van lease slipped to November.[^1]

## Ridership by zone

| Zone        | Q2 trips   | Q3 trips   | Change     | On-time % |
| ----------- | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- | --------- |
| Eastside    | 6,912      | 8,870      | +28.3%     | 91.2      |
| Riverbend   | 4,105      | 4,388      | +6.9%      | 88.7      |
| Hollis Park | 2,760      | 3,011      | +9.1%      | 93.4      |
| **Total**   | **13,777** | **16,269** | **+18.1%** | **90.8**  |

Note: the Total row change differs slightly from the headline figure because 412 Q3 trips were booked by phone and not tagged to a zone until October.

## Risks

- **Driver retention.** Two of nine drivers gave notice in September. We posted both roles on Oct 2; one offer is out.
- **Charging capacity.** The depot has two Level 2 chargers for three EVs. Facilities quoted \$14,800 for a third.
- **Grant timing.** The state mobility grant decision moved from November to _"early Q1"_.

## Decisions requested

1. Approve the \$14,800 charger installation from operating reserve.
2. Approve extending the Hollis Park pilot through June 30, 2027.
3. Ratify the updated fare-free policy for riders 65+ (attached as Appendix B).

[^1]: The lease was signed on October 3; first payment is due November 15.
