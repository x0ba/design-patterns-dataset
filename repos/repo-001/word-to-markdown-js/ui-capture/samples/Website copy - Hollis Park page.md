# Hollis Park On-Demand

Need a ride to the clinic, the grocery store, or the Hollis Park library? Book a Northgate van and we'll pick you up at your door.

## Hours

- Monday–Friday: 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.
- Saturday: 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- Sunday: no service

## Fares

\$2 per ride. **Free for riders 65 and older** (starting November 1) and for children under 12 riding with an adult.

## How to book

1. Download the Northgate Rides app, or call (555) 014-2290.
2. Tell us where you're going and when.
3. We'll send a pickup window of 15 minutes.

Questions? [Email us](mailto:riders@northgatetransit.org) or stop by the office at 410 Mill Street.
