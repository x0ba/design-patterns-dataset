# Rural & Suburban Mobility Innovation Grant — Project Narrative

**Applicant:** Northgate Community Transit Alliance

**Request:** \$412,000 over 24 months

## Statement of need

Roughly 11,400 residents of the Northgate service area live more than half a mile from a fixed-route bus stop, and 23% of households in the Eastside and Riverbend census tracts have no vehicle.[^1] Many of those residents are older adults or people with disabilities for whom a half-mile walk is not realistic. Our 2025 rider survey found that 38% of respondents had skipped a medical appointment in the prior year because they could not get there.[^2]

## Project description

We propose to expand on-demand microtransit from three pilot zones to five, add two wheelchair-accessible electric vans, and integrate trip booking with the county's paratransit call center so that riders have one number to call.

### Goals

1. Increase monthly trips from ~5,500 to 9,000 by month 18.
2. Keep average wait time under 20 minutes at the 85th percentile.
3. Cut paratransit trip denials in the service area by half.
