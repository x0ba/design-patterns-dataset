# Lakeview community meeting

Lakeview Senior Center, Sept 30, 6:30–8:00 p.m. About 45 people attended. Spanish interpretation provided by Rosa M.

## What we heard

- People want to get to the Kroger on Route 9 and the Lakeview clinic most often.
- Several people said the fixed-route bus (Route 14) stops running too early, at 7:15 p.m.
- Concern about whether vans will come down the gravel roads north of Pine Lake. (Greg: yes, but slowly.)
- One resident asked if dogs are allowed. Service animals yes; pets in carriers yes.

## Questions we couldn't answer

1. Will there be a monthly pass? (Board decision — add to January agenda.)
2. Can the van take someone to the Fairmont VA? (Out of zone; see volunteer driver proposal.)
