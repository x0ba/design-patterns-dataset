# Appendix B: Fare-Free Rides for Older Adults

**Effective:** November 1, 2026 · **Policy owner:** Executive Director

## Policy

Riders aged 65 and older ride free on all Northgate microtransit zones at all times. Riders need to verify their age once, by showing any government-issued ID or Medicare card to a driver or at the office.

## Rationale

Riders 65+ made 41% of trips in Q3 but paid only 9% of fare revenue, since most already qualified for the reduced fare. Collecting the remaining fares costs more in driver time and payment processing than it brings in.

## Fiscal impact

Estimated revenue loss: **\$6,800 per year**, offset by roughly \$2,100 in reduced card-processing fees.

## Review

This policy will be reviewed by the board annually, starting October 2027.
