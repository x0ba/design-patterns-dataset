# Proposal: Volunteer Driver Program for Medical Trips

## Problem

Riders who need trips outside our zones (dialysis in Fairmont, specialists at Midstate General) currently have no option except the county's paratransit, which must be booked 7 days ahead.

## Proposal

Pilot a volunteer driver program for out-of-zone medical trips, modeled on the program in Carver County. Volunteers use their own cars and are reimbursed at the IRS rate (70¢/mile in 2026).

## Open questions

- Insurance: does our umbrella policy cover volunteers? (Dana checking with broker)
- Background checks: who pays, and how long do they take?
- How many volunteers do we need for ~30 trips/month?
