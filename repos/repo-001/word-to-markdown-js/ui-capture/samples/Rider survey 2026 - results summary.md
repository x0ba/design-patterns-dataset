# 2026 Rider Survey: Results Summary

We received **702 responses** between June 1 and June 30 (online 61%, phone 27%, paper 12%). Thank you to the volunteers from Lakeview Senior Center who ran the phone banks.

## Headline numbers

| Question                                 | 2025 | 2026 |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---- | ---- |
| Satisfied or very satisfied overall      | 78%  | 84%  |
| Would recommend to a friend              | 81%  | 86%  |
| Waited more than 20 minutes on last trip | 27%  | 19%  |
| Use the service at least weekly          | 44%  | 52%  |

## What riders asked for

1. Earlier weekday service (before 6 a.m.) — 212 mentions
2. Sunday service in Riverbend — 167 mentions
3. Text messages when the van is close — 140 mentions
4. Being able to book a return trip at the same time — 96 mentions

## Selected comments

"The drivers are the best part. Andre always helps me with my groceries." — Eastside rider, 70s

"I wish I could book for my mother from my phone even though the account is hers." — Hollis Park caregiver

"Phone line hold times were long in May." — Riverbend rider

## Next steps

Text alerts are on the Q4 roadmap. Sunday Riverbend service is included in the grant request.
