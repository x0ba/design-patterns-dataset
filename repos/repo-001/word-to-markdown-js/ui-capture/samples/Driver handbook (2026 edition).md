# Northgate Driver Handbook

_2026 edition. Supersedes the March 2025 handbook. Questions go to <operations@northgatetransit.org>._

## 1. Before your shift

1. Arrive 15 minutes early and sign in on the depot tablet.
2. Complete the pre-trip inspection:
   1. Tires, lights, mirrors, and wipers
   2. Wheelchair lift: run one full cycle
   3. First-aid kit and fire extinguisher present and sealed
3. Check the battery is at least 80% (EVs) or the fuel tank is at least half full.
4. Open the driver app and confirm your manifest loads.

## 2. Picking up riders

Wait **five minutes** past the scheduled pickup time before marking a no-show. If a rider uses a mobility device, wait up to ten minutes and call dispatch before leaving.

- Greet riders by name when the manifest has one.
- Offer help with bags; never lift a rider.
- Secure wheelchairs with all four tie-downs _and_ the lap belt.

## 3. Incidents

For any collision, injury, or rider complaint, call dispatch at **(555) 014-2290** first, then fill out the incident form in the app before the end of your shift. Do not discuss fault at the scene.

## 4. Pay and time off

Paychecks are issued every other Friday. Request time off at least two weeks ahead in [the scheduling portal](https://schedule.northgatetransit.org). Shift swaps must be approved by a dispatcher.
