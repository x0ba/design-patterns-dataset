# What we learned from one year of on-demand vans

_By Priya Raman_

When we launched the Eastside pilot last September, we had one van, two drivers, and a spreadsheet. Twelve months later we've given more than 52,000 rides across three neighborhoods. Here's what surprised us.

## 1. The phone matters more than the app

We assumed most riders would book in the app. In practice, 44% of trips are still booked by phone, and those riders are disproportionately the people we most wanted to reach: older adults, people without smartphones, and caregivers booking for someone else.

## 2. Short trips are the point

The median trip is **2.3 miles**. These are trips that are too far to walk with a cane and too short to justify a taxi: the pharmacy, the senior center, the bus transfer at Mill & 4th.

## 3. Drivers are the product

Our survey comments mention drivers by name more often than anything else. We now pay drivers \$2/hour above the regional median, and turnover dropped from 60% to 22%.

## What's next

We're applying for state funding to add two more zones and connect with county paratransit. If you live in Lakeview or Ashford and want to help shape that, [sign up for a community meeting](https://northgatetransit.org/meetings).
