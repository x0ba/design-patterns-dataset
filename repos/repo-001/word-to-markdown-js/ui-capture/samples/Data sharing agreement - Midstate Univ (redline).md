# Data Sharing Agreement

Between Northgate Community Transit Alliance ("Northgate") and the Board of Trustees of Midstate University ("University").

## 1. Purpose

Northgate will provide trip-level data to the University's Urban Mobility Lab solely for evaluating the microtransit program described in the grant application dated September 2026.

## 2. Data provided

- Trip records: origin and destination ~~address~~ **census block group**, request time, pickup time, drop-off time, zone
- Rider attributes: age band and mobility-device flag only. **No names, phone numbers, or addresses.**

## 3. Security

The University will store the data on encrypted, access-controlled systems and limit access to named research staff. Data will be deleted within 90 days after the final evaluation report.

## 4. Publication

The University may publish aggregate findings. Northgate will have 30 days to review any publication for confidential information[^1] before submission.

## 5. Term

This agreement runs from the date of last signature through December 31, 2028.

[^1]: Review is for confidentiality only; Northgate cannot block publication of findings.
