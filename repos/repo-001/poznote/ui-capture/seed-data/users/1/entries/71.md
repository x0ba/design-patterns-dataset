# Kanelbullar

## Ingredients
- 500 g flour
- 75 g sugar
- 25 g yeast
- 300 ml milk
- 75 g butter
- 1 tsp cardamom. Filling: 100 g butter
- 75 g sugar
- 2 tbsp cinnamon.

## Method
Knead 10 min, rise 45 min. Roll, spread filling, fold in thirds, cut strips, twist and knot. Rise 30 min, egg wash, pearl sugar, 225 °C for 8–10 min.
