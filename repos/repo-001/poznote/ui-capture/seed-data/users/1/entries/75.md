# Sourdough — current recipe

## Ingredients
- 450 g bread flour
- 50 g whole wheat
- 360 g water
- 100 g starter
- 10 g salt.

## Method
Autolyse 1h. Add starter + salt. 4 sets of folds every 30 min. Bulk until +60% (usually 5h at 23 °C). Shape, cold retard 14h. Bake 250 °C lid on 20 min, 230 °C lid off 22 min.
