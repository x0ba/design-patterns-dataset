# Weeknight dal

## Ingredients
- 200 g red lentils
- 1 onion
- ginger
- garlic
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 can coconut milk
- spinach
- lime.

## Method
Rinse lentils, simmer with turmeric 15 min. Fry onion/ginger/garlic, add to lentils with coconut milk, 10 more min. Stir in spinach, finish with lime and tadka (cumin seeds + chilli in ghee).
