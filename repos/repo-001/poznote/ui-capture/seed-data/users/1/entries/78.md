# Lamb tagine for guests

## Ingredients
- 1 kg lamb shoulder
- 2 onions
- ras el hanout
- dried apricots
- chickpeas
- preserved lemon
- almonds.

## Method
Brown lamb, soften onions, spices, add stock and apricots. 2h at 160 °C, add chickpeas for the last 30 min. Serve with couscous and herb yoghurt. Feeds 6.
