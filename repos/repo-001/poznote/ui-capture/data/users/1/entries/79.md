# Pepperkaker

## Ingredients
- 150 g butter
- 150 g sugar
- 1 dl golden syrup
- 1 dl cream
- spices (cinnamon, ginger, cloves, pepper)
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ~500 g flour.

## Method
Melt butter, sugar, syrup. Cool, add cream, spices, soda, flour. Rest overnight. Roll thin, 175 °C for 6–8 min. Makes ~120.
