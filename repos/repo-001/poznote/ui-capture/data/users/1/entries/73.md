# Fiskesuppe (Bergen style)

## Ingredients
- 400 g cod + salmon
- 1 carrot
- 1 leek
- ½ celeriac
- 1 l fish stock
- 3 dl cream
- 1 dl crème fraîche
- chives.

## Method
Julienne the veg, simmer in stock 5 min. Add cream, bring to simmer, add fish in chunks, poach 4 min off heat. Season with a little sugar and vinegar(!). Chives.
